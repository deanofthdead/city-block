CITYBLOCK

Draw the roads. Build the blocks.

Play it like an app: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Still in the deck

    How to play

    1. Draw 3 roads on the empty lot, each up to 9 squares. A road is one stroke that can bend, both ends must reach the map edge or another road, and once drawn it's final. Every road gets sidewalks along it, and the lot inside them becomes city blocks.
    2. Cards are dealt two at a time, with a random build area: a 4×4 square or a 2-wide row or column. Build one card inside the area; the other is thrown away. 24 pairs from a 48-card deck.
    3. Buildings and parks go inside blocks. Hot dog carts and newsstands go on sidewalks. Taxis go on roads.
    4. When the deck runs out, every tile scores. Plan for what's still to come.
